What to know

  • Box office figures come from Sacnilk, Box Office Mojo, The Numbers and trade press, verified by a second pass. All dollar figures convert at ₹93 per dollar.
  • Search data is Google Trends film topics (all searches for the film, any wording), India and worldwide, measured up to 8 days before release.
  • Wednesday 4 Nov paid previews are folded into Day 1, the way Sacnilk reports them.
  • Ramayana's overseas pre-sales are Sacnilk's $400K opening day plus $550K North America previews. The widely quoted $2M figure includes India.
  • The forecast moves with bookings: rerun the model with each new Sacnilk snapshot before release.
