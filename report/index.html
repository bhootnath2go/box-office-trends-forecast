How the forecast is built

Worldwide opening gross per day = regression on worldwide Google Trends interest, Hollywood comps only. Weekend = per day × 4 (Thu–Sun). Day 1 = the Hollywood opening-day share of a 3-day weekend. India is the home market, at the Hollywood median home share, capped each day at what its screens can physically sell.

What to know

  • Box office figures come from Sacnilk, Box Office Mojo, The Numbers and trade press, verified by a second pass. All dollar figures convert at ₹93 per dollar.
  • Search data is Google Trends film topics (all searches for the film, any wording), worldwide, as a multiple of a steady reference term, measured up to 8 days before release.
  • Day 1 includes previews, as Hollywood opening days do.
  • The earlier bookings-based forecast is kept in the repo's model output for reference only; it does not feed these numbers.
